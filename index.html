03 / Approach How we work

A structured engineering workflow — from first question to a bankable build.

Four phases, each with defined deliverables. You always know what you'll receive, when you'll receive it, and what the next phase will cost to commission.

PHASE 01

Scoping

Define the project, the constraints, the standards it must meet, and the deliverables expected at each gate.

PHASE 02

Studies

Prefeasibility, site investigation, resource assessment, and impact studies — test assumptions before capital is committed.

PHASE 03

Engineering

Detailed design, drawings, BOQs, modelling, and bankability documentation built to standards investors and regulators recognise.

PHASE 04

Delivery

Construction supervision, commissioning support, and team training. We hand over a project your operations team can actually run.
